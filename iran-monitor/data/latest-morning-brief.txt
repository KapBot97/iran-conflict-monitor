IRAN CONFLICT INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
MORNING | MARCH 30, 2026 | DAY 31

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The conflict entered Day 31 with Trump issuing his most expansive threat yet — publicly threatening to destroy Iran's oil wells, Kharg Island, all power plants, and "possibly" desalination infrastructure if a deal is not reached "shortly," a marked escalation from prior power-plant ultimatums. Brent crude moved to $115.35 (+$2.78 vs last evening brief at $112.57) on the rhetoric, with the Kharg Island threat adding a new destruction-of-supply dimension the market had not fully priced. Overnight strikes hit Tehran power infrastructure (blackout since restored), Karaj, Tabriz petrochemical (fire contained), Shiraz, Qom, and Abadan. The diplomatic window via Pakistan remains open but structurally thin — Israel publicly confirmed it will not pause strikes ahead of any US-Iran talks, and Iran's parliament speaker accused Washington of planning a ground invasion while publicly "negotiating."

SITUATION BY THEATER
- IRAN MAINLAND: Overnight IDF strikes hit Tehran power infrastructure causing multi-hour blackout (since restored per Iranian authorities); explosions confirmed in Ray (Greater Tehran metro), Karaj, Shiraz, Qom, Abadan, and Tabriz — Tabriz petrochemical plant caught fire, contained per Fars. IDF separately struck an IRGC-affiliated university in Tehran on Monday, claiming it was used for advanced weapons research. IRGC retaliatory deadline for US/Israeli universities in region was 12:00 noon Tehran time Monday (passed without confirmed strike). Two MEK-linked individuals executed. Iran internet blackout now 30 consecutive days. Trump told FT he wants to "take the oil in Iran" and could seize Kharg Island; claimed Iran has already undergone "regime change" with Khamenei's death. DELTA: Trump's desalination/oil-wells/Kharg threat is new — prior scope was limited to power plants. BLU-91/B Gator anti-tank mines reportedly found near Shiraz villages, assessed by analysts as IAF-dispersed (single-source, see confidence).

- ISRAEL/NORTHERN FRONT: IDF continued Beirut southern suburb strikes, ordering civilian evacuations, claiming Hezbollah military targets. Iraq Baghdad airport area: blasts reported from capital. Iran-backed Houthis have entered the conflict firing missiles at Israel. ADAMA (Syngenta subsidiary) Makhteshim plant in southern Israel hit by Iranian missile or debris — no casualties. Israel vowing to seize additional Lebanon territory. Cumulative death toll: 2,000+ across the region; Iran alone 1,200+ killed per Iranian Red Crescent.

- GULF STATES: UAE cumulative: 414 ballistic missiles, 15 cruise missiles, 1,914 UAVs engaged since Feb 28 — Sunday alone saw 16 ballistic missiles + 42 drones intercepted. Two UAE military, one Moroccan contractor, eight civilians killed total; 178 injured. UAE Minister called for full Arab League solidarity or "paralysis." Saudi Arabia: 5 ballistic missiles headed toward Eastern Province intercepted March 30. Saudi FM joined emergency GCC-Russia-Jordan video conference on Iranian attacks. Kuwait: power/desalination plant struck in what Kuwait called "sinful Iranian aggression" — one Indian worker killed. Bahrain: third siren activation in four hours. Emirati minister calling for Iran accountability and material damages. DELTA: Kuwait infrastructure confirmed hit; Saudi intercept count rising; GCC-Russia consultative meeting is new.

- STRAIT OF HORMUZ: Traffic remains severely depressed — tanker transits running approximately 2 per day (TankerTrackers), roughly 70% below pre-conflict norms. Half of transiting vessels carry Iranian oil/refined products — Iran is effectively running a selective "toll booth" on Hormuz, charging some vessels millions to transit. 150+ ships anchoring outside strait. A slight uptick in transits late last week did not hold. Iran has made 21+ confirmed attacks on merchant ships as of mid-March. "Tehran toll booth" dynamic means even nominal transits carry seizure/extortion risk. DELTA: No material improvement in traffic; Iran's selective passage policy entrenches asymmetry.

- IRAQ/PROXY THEATER: Baghdad airport area reported blasts Monday. Russia providing satellite imagery of US-UK Diego Garcia base, Incirlik (Turkey), Al Udeid (Qatar), and Shaybah oil field (Saudi Arabia) to Iran per ISW/WaPo reporting — suggesting Russia actively enabling Iranian targeting. PMF and Iraqi proxy activity ongoing. US has now struck 4 key Iranian ballistic missile production facilities and 29 missile launch bases cumulatively.

DIPLOMATIC TRACK
Status: Active but low-credibility. Pakistan hosted Gulf+Turkey/Egypt FM summit Sunday and is preparing to host "meaningful US-Iran talks in coming days" in Islamabad. Pakistani FM Ishaq Dar confirmed talks covered ceasefire pathways. Iran has submitted a five-point counter-proposal to the US plan; Iranian FM described US ceasefire terms as "one-sided and unfair." Tehran simultaneously accuses Washington of planning a ground invasion. Israel publicly confirmed via unnamed official to Reuters that it will NOT scale back strikes ahead of any potential Washington-Tehran dialogue. Trump said Sunday "We'll make a deal, I'm pretty sure" while hours later threatening to obliterate Kharg Island. Spain banning US military aircraft from Rota/Moron bases and closing airspace to Operation Epic Fury planes — forcing relocation of 15 US aircraft, creating minor NATO friction. EU extended human rights sanctions on Iran. ASSESSMENT: Talks remain structurally blocked. No mechanism, no agreed mediator, no Iranian principal willing to negotiate. Pakistan channel is the only live wire; probability of a ceasefire framework before April 6 remains below 10%.

MARKET IMPLICATIONS
- Brent upside scenario: Trump's Kharg Island + oil wells threat, if executed, would remove 1.5-2.0 mb/d of Iranian exports permanently and push Brent toward $130-140; current $115 reflects partial pricing of that tail — watch for any Trump operational order or IDF asset repositioning toward Kharg as the trigger.
- Demand destruction emerging: JavierBlas/Bloomberg analysis flags demand destruction beginning to bite at $115 Brent — industrial demand slowdown in Asia, IEA emergency reserve releases ongoing; ceiling may be $125-130 before demand-side cap limits further upside absent Hormuz full closure or Kharg destruction.
- Rate/equities: Stagflationary impulse intensifying — energy-driven CPI spike plus slowing global trade (Hormuz chokehold, Spain-US NATO friction) complicates Fed path; defense/energy overweight vs consumer discretionary/airlines remains the positioning call; sovereign credit stress in energy-import-dependent EMs (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh) widening.

SEMICONDUCTOR / SUPPLY CHAIN
- Helium: Qatar is the world's second-largest helium producer; Al Udeid base targeting (Russian satellite imagery) and ongoing regional instability elevates supply disruption risk for semiconductor fabs reliant on Qatari helium — no confirmed Qatar LNG/helium infrastructure strike yet but the targeting intelligence is live.
- Qatar LNG: Major chipmakers (TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix) rely on stable LNG supply chains through Qatar; any Qatar infrastructure strike would ripple into Asian power costs within 60-90 days.
- Global shipping: Hormuz disruption has already extended Asian manufacturing input lead times; Apple, automotive, and specialty chemical supply chains absorbing 3-6 week delays on Gulf-routed components.
- Spain/US basing friction: Rota/Moron denial forces B-52 and ISR rerouting through UK/Diego Garcia, adding operational complexity but not degrading strike capability materially.

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE: Trump intends Kharg Island seizure as a military objective (FT interview context suggests serious planning, not just rhetoric); Russia providing Iran with satellite targeting data for US/GCC bases (ISW sourcing, corroborated by Zelensky statement); Brent repricing toward $120+ if Kharg threat is operationalized within 72 hours; IDF will not pause strikes ahead of Pakistan-mediated talks (unnamed Israeli official to Reuters).
